# Product Overview

## Church Family Youth Movement Registration System

A comprehensive web application for managing youth membership and activities at The Apostolic Church Nigeria - IJU Station Area.

### Core Purpose
- Youth member registration and profile management
- Attendance tracking and spiritual growth monitoring
- Department assignment and ministry participation
- Administrative oversight and member analytics
- Community engagement and fellowship coordination

### Key Features
- **Member Registration**: Complete profile creation with personal, spiritual, and contact information
- **Ranking System**: Four-tier spiritual advancement tracking (S-Servant, A-Chosen, B-Called, C-Multitude)
- **Department Management**: Five ministry departments (Music, Ushering, Drama, Clean Up, Intercessory)
- **Family Organization**: Three spiritual families (Eden, Taberah, Bethel) for community structure
- **Mandatory Registration**: Reservation number required for all new member registrations
- **Attendance Tracking**: Event participation monitoring and history
- **Admin Portal**: Comprehensive member management and analytics dashboard with delete capabilities
- **Profile Management**: Photo uploads, personal information updates, and spiritual journey tracking

### Target Users
- **Youth Members**: Ages typically 13-35, seeking spiritual growth and community involvement
- **Church Administrators**: Leaders managing member data, attendance, and ministry assignments
- **Department Heads**: Ministry leaders overseeing specific service areas

### Church Context
- Part of The Apostolic Church Nigeria denomination
- Focused on youth activism, community service, and spiritual development
- Emphasizes both worship and practical community engagement
